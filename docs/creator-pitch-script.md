# OpticTrader — Content Creator Pitch Script

A script you can read (or paraphrase) when reaching out to a trading content creator about promoting OpticTrader. The first section is the actual talking points; the numbers appendix at the bottom is for your own quick reference during the call/DM.

---

## The pitch

> Hey — I run OpticTrader, a trading journal web app. I'm reaching out because I think your audience would actually use it, and I want to set you up with your own discount code so you earn from anyone who signs up through you.

**What it is:**
> OpticTrader is a trading journal — but instead of just logging wins and losses, it tracks your setups, your emotional state, whether you followed your own rules, and your entry/exit timing. It's got a calendar that shows your logging streak, a dashboard with win rate and equity curve, and an AI Coach that gives feedback based on *your* actual trading history — not generic advice. It works with any market — futures, forex, crypto, stocks — not tied to one instrument.

**Why traders like it:**
> Most traders either don't journal at all, or use a spreadsheet they abandon after a week. OpticTrader is built to actually make you *want* to log — it's fast, it looks good, and the AI Coach makes the data useful instead of just sitting there.

**The trial:**
> Everyone gets 5 days free, no card required. So there's zero friction for your audience to try it.

**Your code:**
> I'll set you up with your own code — say it's `[CREATORCODE]`. Anyone who uses it gets 25% off, *forever*, on either plan. That's a better deal than the public discount code, which is only 25% off the first month. So it's a genuine perk for your audience, not just a tracking link.

**What's in it for you:**
> You get 20% of the discounted monthly price for every person who signs up through your code and stays on the Monthly plan — that's $6/month, recurring, for as long as they stay subscribed. Your own dashboard shows signups, who's converted to paying, and your commission rate in real time.

**The honest caveat (say this — don't skip it):**
> Full disclosure: real billing isn't live yet on the platform, so right now your dashboard tracks signups and attribution, not actual payouts. That's coming — once it's live, the commission structure above kicks in for real. I want you in early because the terms only get better for early creators, not worse.

**Community:**
> There's also a Discord — [invite link] — for traders using the app, and it's a good place for your audience to land after signing up. Feel free to drop your link there too.

**Ask:**
> If you're in, send me your preferred code name and I'll get it live on your account today, along with your creator dashboard link.
